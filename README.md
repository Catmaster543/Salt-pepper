# What is this mod? ❔

This mod adds **salt** and **pepper** to minecraft, which you can use to improve your food. Applying either salt, pepper or both, improves the saturation values of the food it is applied on. Salt and pepper can be put into shakers.

***

## **Shakers**

You can make an empty shaker with an iron nugget and glass. You can then fill up the shaker with either; salt, or pepper. With each refill you add extra 8 uses to the shaker, with a maximum of 64, meaning you can season a whole stack of food at once. To use the filled shaker, you just pick it up in your inventory and right click any food item to season it.

## **Salt**

You can obtain salt underground in its stone, or even more rarely deepslate form. 
Upon breaking the rock salt you gain rock salt which you can then shove into a furnace and get usable salt to make your food reeal salty.
Or you can insert it inside a shaker, which will fill it by 8.

## **Pepper**

To find pepper you will need to venture into the jungle, where you can find a **pepper pod**, which behaves similar to a cocoa pod. 
Upon breaking it you get seeds and/or green peppercorns.
You can plant the seed on a jungle tree just like a cocoa pod to grow more.
Now you can't do much with the green peppercorns themselves, you first need to boil them. 
Boiling can be done by either placing a campfire underneath a caulron filled with water and right clicking it with the green peppercorns, or using the cooking pot from farmers delight. 
Once boiled you get blanched peppercorns. Similar to the salt you now need to put these in a furnace to get black peppercorns. 
And lastly you need to break them down into fine powder, which you can do with the power of your own fist (your inventory crafting).
With the pepper broken down you can use it directly in your crafting grid to flavor any food, or put in a shaker and fill it up by 8 again.

***

## **Versions**

Currently the mod is available for Neoforge 1.21.1 & 26.1.2, Fabric 1.20.1, 1.21.1, 26.1, 26.1.1 & 26.1.2 & Forge 1.20.1. No other versions are planned (outsidene major versions) unless someone would specifically request them.

## **Compatibility ✔️**

This mod should be completely compatible with any other food mods out there.
You can use Farmer's Delight to add a way to boil salt, which is by using a cooking pot if it installed.
